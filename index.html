Three laws run everything you see

Johannes Kepler worked these out from Tycho Brahe's naked-eye records in the early 1600s. The lab solves them, live, for every planet on every frame.

LAW I

Orbits are ellipses

The Sun sits at one focus, not the centre. The planet swings closest at perihelion and farthest at aphelion.

r = a(1 − e²) / (1 + e cos ν)
LAW II

Equal areas in equal times

Each coloured wedge takes the same time to sweep. Near the Sun the planet races to cover it; far out it crawls.

M = E − e·sin E
LAW III

Farther out, slower and longer

A planet's year squared grows with its distance cubed. All eight planets fall on one straight line.

T² ∝ a³
