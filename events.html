Sky Events Calendar

Eclipses, meteor showers, planets meeting in the sky, oppositions, supermoons and the seasons for the next 12 months. Every planet and Moon event is calculated in your browser, then checked against your city: what time to look, which direction and how high.

ग्रहण, उल्का वर्षा, ग्रहों का मिलन और सुपरमून: अपने शहर से कब और किस दिशा में देखें।

How this is computed: planet positions use JPL Keplerian elements and the Moon uses a low-precision lunar theory, accurate to a few minutes for timings. Eclipse dates and types are from the NASA Eclipse Web Site (Fred Espenak); whether each one is visible from your city, and how much of the Sun is covered, is calculated here and is approximate. Meteor shower peaks and rates follow the International Meteor Organization calendar; real rates under city lights are far lower than the ideal rates shown. Times are IST.
