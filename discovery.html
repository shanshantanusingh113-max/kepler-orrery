Discovery Engine

Links observations of the same quantity from different missions and instruments, finds excursions and disagreements, then tests each one: is it replicated, does it follow a processing version, is it confounded by when it was observed? Every finding ends with a specific question to investigate next.

Engine settings

Excursions use robust z-scores against the same instrument's own baseline: its site history for landers and rovers, or a robust latitude-trend fit for orbiters. Disagreements use the paired difference divided by the combined measurement error.

How a verdict is reached

Likely real when independent instruments see it, it holds across processing versions, and it spans several observations.
Likely artifact when it tracks a processing version, shows the same offset everywhere, or rests on one point that another instrument contradicts.
Unresolved when the instruments that could confirm it never observed under the same conditions.
