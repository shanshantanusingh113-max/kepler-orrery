    Tips

    • Get away from street lights and look for an open horizon.
    • Give your eyes 10 to 15 minutes to adapt, and use a red light, not your phone screen.
    • Planets shine with a steady light; stars twinkle.
    • Binoculars show Jupiter's four big moons and the Moon's craters.
