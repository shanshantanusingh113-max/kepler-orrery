Tool 01 · Image change detection

Planetary Anomaly Hunter

Compares two images of the same patch of Mars, the Moon or an asteroid. It registers them, normalizes brightness, finds changes, screens out sensor artifacts, and ranks what is left with a confidence score. It also scans a single image for rare textures.

Image source

A synthetic Mars-like pair with known injected changes and one detector stripe, so you can check what the hunter finds against ground truth.

Earlier image
Later image

Both images must cover the same area at a similar scale. Offsets up to 8 pixels at working size are corrected automatically. Images are processed in your browser and never uploaded anywhere.

Detection settings

σ is a robust standard deviation of the difference image (median absolute deviation), so a few large changes do not inflate the noise estimate.
